Two Friends and a Quiet Cave.

Do you remember the angel Jibreel, who came to Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, in the cave of Hira and said “Iqra”?
That is how Allah sent His messages to the Prophet: through Jibreel, a very special angel made of light.
Many years later, Jibreel came again with a new message from Allah: “It is time to move to a new city called Madinah.”
The people of Madinah were waiting to welcome him.
So in the middle of a hot afternoon, when the streets were empty, the Prophet, peace be upon him, went to the house of his best friend, Abu Bakr, and told him the news.
Abu Bakr asked, “May I come with you?”
The Prophet, peace be upon him, said, “Yes.”
Abu Bakr was so happy that tears of joy rolled down his cheeks.
Before leaving, the Prophet asked his young cousin Ali to stay behind for a little while and return everything people had left with him for safekeeping.
Even while leaving, Al-Amin made sure everyone got their things back.
The two friends left quietly at night, under a sky full of stars.
They walked to a mountain called Thawr and found a small cave near the top.
Inside it was cool and quiet.
Abu Bakr cleaned it carefully so his friend could rest comfortably.
Their friends helped them.
Abu Bakr’s daughter, Asma, brought them food.
A kind shepherd named Aamir brought his sheep near the cave every evening, so they could drink fresh, warm milk.
Then the sheep walked over the path, and their little hoofprints covered the friends’ footsteps.
One day, some people came up the mountain looking for them and walked very close to the cave.
Abu Bakr whispered, “If they look down at their feet, they will see us!”
But Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, stayed calm.
He gently said: “Don’t be sad.
Allah is with us.”
Soon the people turned around and went back down the mountain.
They never looked inside.
After three nights, the two friends continued their journey.
In Madinah, people had been waiting every morning, watching from rooftops and palm trees.
When they saw him coming, everyone cheered with joy, and children ran out smiling to welcome him.
He was safe, among friends who loved him.
Tomorrow night: a camel that chose a very special place in Madinah.
