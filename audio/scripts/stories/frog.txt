Eat the Frog First.

Once upon a time, in a cozy little village surrounded by green meadows and singing birds, there lived three young friends.
Their names were Leo the Lion cub, Bella the Bunny, and Marco the Monkey.
Every single morning, the three friends had the same challenge.
Each of them had to eat one slimy, slippery frog before they could enjoy their day.
The frog was not delicious at all. It was their least favourite thing in the whole world.
But it was important, and it simply had to be done.
Leo the Lion cub always said the same thing. I will eat it later.
He played and ran and rolled in the soft grass all morning long.
But every single hour, he thought about that waiting frog, and it made all his fun feel a little less fun.
By afternoon, he was tired and worried, and eating the frog felt twice as hard.
He went to bed feeling like he had not quite finished his day properly.
Bella the Bunny was even worse.
She kept staring at the frog, then quickly looking away.
Maybe tomorrow, she whispered nervously.
But tomorrow came, and there were two frogs waiting!
She hopped in anxious circles until one day she had a whole mountain of frogs, and no idea where to begin.
But Marco the Monkey had discovered a wonderful secret.
Every single morning, the very first thing he did was eat the frog.
He would make a very funny disgusted face. But then, that was it. Done. Finished.
The entire rest of his day was completely free and bright and joyful.
He swung through the trees, played with friends, and went to sleep every night feeling proud and peaceful.
How are you always so happy? asked Leo one day.
Marco smiled his biggest smile and said, When you do the hardest thing first, everything else feels wonderfully easy.
The frog does not get bigger when you eat it early. But in your imagination, it grows like a terrible monster when you keep waiting.
From that day forward, Leo and Bella tried Marco's way.
And slowly, their days became lighter, brighter, and full of real and lasting joy.
So remember: whatever feels hard and uncomfortable, do it first.
Eat the frog early, and the rest of your whole day will taste absolutely wonderful.
The End.
