Sindbad the Sailor.

Once upon a time, in the great city of Baghdad, there lived a young poor porter named Hindbad.
One very hot day, feeling tired, he rested outside a grand palace where beautiful music drifted through the windows.
The palace belonged to Sindbad the Sailor, who invited him inside and told him the story of his seven amazing voyages.
In his first voyage, Sindbad sailed with merchants across the wide blue sea.
They stopped to rest on what they thought was a beautiful tropical island.
But it was actually an enormous sleeping whale!
When they lit a fire to cook their food, the giant creature suddenly woke up and dove deep into the ocean.
Sindbad clung to a wooden plank and was eventually rescued by a passing ship.
On his second voyage, Sindbad discovered a magical valley filled with giant sparkling diamonds, guarded by enormous serpents.
He cleverly tied himself to a piece of meat that a hungry eagle carried up from the valley, and escaped with his pockets full of diamonds.
His third voyage brought him face to face with a terrifying one-eyed giant called a Cyclops.
Sindbad and his brave friends outsmarted the creature using burning torches and escaped on a raft they built themselves.
During his fourth voyage, Sindbad was buried alive inside a dark underground tomb.
But his clever mind helped him discover a secret way out, and he returned home wealthier than before.
On his fifth voyage, he encountered the mighty Roc, a bird so enormous its egg was as big as a house!
He faced terrible danger but escaped using his quick thinking.
His sixth voyage brought him to a magical island of extraordinary riches.
On his seventh and final voyage, he was captured by a strange creature who refused to leave his shoulders.
Through great patience and cleverness, Sindbad finally tricked the creature and freed himself forever.
After all seven incredible adventures, Sindbad returned to Baghdad, wealthy, wise, and deeply grateful.
He shared his fortune with Hindbad and taught him the greatest lesson of all.
Courage, kindness, and a clever mind can overcome any obstacle in the world.
And they both lived happily ever after. The End.
