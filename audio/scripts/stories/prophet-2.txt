The Cloak That Stopped a Fight.

Long ago in Makkah, there was a very special building called the Kaaba.
It was a house built for worshipping Allah, first built by Prophet Ibrahim and his son Ismail.
One year, heavy rain came, and a big flood rushed through the valley.
The walls of the Kaaba were damaged.
So all the tribes of Makkah decided to rebuild it together.
Everyone carried stones, and the walls rose higher and higher.
In one corner of the Kaaba there was a special stone, the Black Stone.
When the wall was ready, it was time to put the Black Stone back in its place.
But then there was trouble.
Every tribe said, “We should put it there!
It’s our honour!”
The arguing got louder and angrier.
For four or five days nobody agreed, and the men were ready to fight.
Then a wise old man said, “Let’s do this: the first person who walks through the gate tomorrow morning will decide for us.”
Everyone agreed.
The next morning they waited and watched the gate.
And who walked in first?
Muhammad, peace be upon him!
Everyone cheered: “It’s Al-Amin, the Trustworthy One!
We are happy with him!”
Muhammad, peace be upon him, listened to the problem.
Then he asked for a large cloth.
He spread it on the ground and placed the Black Stone in the middle with his own hands.
Then he said, “Let the leader of each tribe hold one edge of the cloth.”
The leaders held the edges and lifted together, carrying the stone all the way to the corner.
Then Muhammad, peace be upon him, picked it up and set it in its place.
Every tribe shared the honour.
Nobody was left out, and nobody had to fight.
That day, everyone saw how a clever, fair idea could turn anger into peace.
Tomorrow night: a secret journey at night, and a cave where enemies came very close.
